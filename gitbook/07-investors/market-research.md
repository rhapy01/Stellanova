# NovaMarket: Market and Competitive Research

**Status:** Initial desk scan · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-28 · **Market sizing:** not established

This page records a narrow initial scan of primary sources. It is not a complete competitor audit, market-size study, or proof of user demand. Product descriptions below are attributed to the organizations that publish them.

## NovaMarket's testable position

**Hypothesis:** NovaMarket can differentiate through a connected social art workflow rather than through a standalone mint button: creators make or upload art, publish it into a social feed, build a public creator identity, configure a collection/drop, and connect social discovery to collecting. Separate services, physical-art, and exhibition experiences extend the creator product beyond NFT listings.

The hypothesis is not validated by the number of implemented features. Evidence should show that a selected creator segment prefers or completes the connected workflow, returns to it, and produces additional verified Stellar activity.

## Research questions

1. Which creator segment experiences the most costly friction today: AI-assisted artists, pixel artists, digital illustrators, mural/painter creators, or another segment?
2. How do creators currently move from making work to publishing, audience building, drops, and sales? Which steps are actually painful?
3. Which Stellar and non-Stellar alternatives already provide social publishing, profiles, creator tooling, minting, and marketplace workflows together?
4. Do collectors discover works through social feeds, collection pages, external links, or marketplace filters?
5. Which outcomes would make creators switch or add NovaMarket: audience reach, simpler publishing, lower cost, trust, creator control, or something else?
6. What new Stellar transactions would NovaMarket cause that would not otherwise occur, and how will those transactions be verified?

## Research plan

- Build a dated feature matrix from official product pages and live product walkthroughs; record the observed date and link for each claim.
- Interview creators and collectors using neutral questions about their current workflows, not leading questions about NovaMarket.
- Record participant segment, recruitment source, interview date, and anonymized finding; do not publish personal data without consent.
- Run a usability study of the standard collection journey and the art-to-feed-to-collect journey.
- Separate founder, tester, and general-user activity under the founder's stated classification rule before reporting any metrics.
- Size the reachable market from a transparent bottom-up method only after target segment and willingness-to-pay evidence exist.

## Market-size and traction policy

No TAM/SAM/SOM, active-user count, retention figure, revenue, or market-share claim is asserted in this document. Use dated primary data, show the calculation, explain exclusions, and state uncertainty. Testnet activity can demonstrate product behavior and ecosystem transactions, but it is not Mainnet liquidity or real-value revenue. Client-submitted analytics events are not verified human adoption.
