# NovaMarket: Investor Plan (Evidence-First Draft)

**Audience:** potential funders and strategic partners · **Status:** working draft, not an offer or award application

## Investment thesis

NovaMarket is building a social art marketplace on Stellar that connects making and publishing artwork with creator discovery, collection creation, minting, and collecting. Its product thesis is that creators and collectors benefit when social discovery and NFT commerce are part of one coherent experience, with Stellar used for verifiable ownership and supported marketplace transactions.

The product has substantial implementation in place, including social surfaces, four creative studios, Stellar Testnet NFT and marketplace flows, and initial creator-commerce workflows. The funding case still needs evidence that this connected experience solves a meaningful user problem, creates differentiated Stellar usage, and can support a sustainable business.

## What exists and what remains unproven

| Is there a business model? | Marketplace and creator-commerce economics are hypotheses | Fee schedule, willingness to pay, provider costs, support/operations cost, and downside financial model |

## Market and alternatives

NovaMarket does not enter an empty Stellar NFT market. Litemint describes a wallet-first Stellar NFT marketplace and states that Stellar NFTs minted elsewhere can work in its market ([Litemint product update](https://blog.litemint.com/posts/wallet-first-nft-marketplace-live/)). The Stellar Development Foundation's historical account of Litemint also describes a Stellar NFT marketplace connecting artists and gamers ([SDF ecosystem article](https://stellar.org/blog/ecosystem/looking-back-on-the-stellar-community-fund-with-litemint)). These sources establish that marketplace and NFT creation activity already exists on Stellar; they do not provide current market size or comparative performance.

**Differentiation hypothesis:** NovaMarket may stand out through the connected social art workflow: create/upload, publish to a feed, build a creator audience, configure a collection/drop, and collect/trade from the same product. The product must demonstrate that people use these connections and that the workflow is meaningfully better for a defined creator segment.

**Research still required:** current competitor capability matrix; creator interviews; collector interviews; pricing/fee comparisons; market sizing with transparent source/method; and evaluation of adjacent social creator products. No TAM/SAM/SOM figure is asserted here.

## Business model hypotheses

Potential revenue sources to evaluate include marketplace transaction fees or royalties where the configured contract supports them, and paid creator-service or physical-art workflows where a platform fee is intentionally implemented. The repository does not establish a verified revenue stream, finalized pricing, realized revenue, or positive unit economics. Artwork tips should not be described as platform revenue unless a documented fee is actually charged.

Before forecasting, document:

- current contract fee/royalty settings and who receives each amount;
- which activity is a primary mint, secondary sale, offer, transfer, tip, service request, or physical-art order;
- provider costs, network costs, support costs, and payment failure/refund costs;
- creator and collector willingness to pay and expected repeat behavior;
- downside, base, and upside scenarios, with formulas and source dates.

## Go-to-market hypothesis

Initial acquisition should focus on creators who can publish art and bring an audience into the product, while the integrated feed and public creator/collection pages help visitors discover additional work. Creator communities and Stellar ecosystem channels are potential distribution partners, not confirmed partnerships. A launch plan should specify one primary creator segment, onboarding support, a repeatable publishing cadence, and a metric for successful creator activation.

## Stellar ecosystem value

The engineering scope uses Stellar Testnet, Soroban contracts, wallet-authorized transactions, and event indexing. A credible ecosystem case should tie funded deliverables to measurable additional Stellar activity, such as confirmed creator mints and settled marketplace transactions, with founder/tester activity disclosed and chain records linked. Integration with an SDK/provider alone is not ecosystem impact.

## Funding and financial readiness

No funding amount, allocation, terms, revenue forecast, or runway is published in this working public draft. Before using an investor or grant version, the founder should approve the funding ask and a costed milestone budget, including any technical co-founder or engineering capacity, infrastructure, security review, and user validation. A public dossier should not contain confidential compensation or personal financial details.

## Investor diligence checklist

- [ ] Current Testnet demo and transaction references for creation, mint, listing, purchase, and relevant creator-commerce flows.
- [ ] Definitions and reconciliation evidence for users, repeat use, mints, sales, marketplace volume, tips, and referrals.
- [ ] Competitor matrix based on current primary sources and user interviews.
- [ ] Sourced market research and transparent calculation method; no unsupported market-size claims.
- [ ] Founder-approved product positioning and target creator segment.
- [ ] Costed roadmap with acceptance criteria and assigned capacity.
- [ ] Financial model with explicit assumptions and downside case.
- [ ] Security review scope, contract version/deployment matrix, and operational recovery evidence.
- [ ] Data-room index with public, investor-confidential, and internal materials clearly separated.

## Claims policy

Do not claim a funding award, partnership, third-party audit, Mainnet readiness, market leadership, broad user adoption, revenue, or a unique capability without evidence. Treat Testnet activity as Testnet activity. Label plans and hypotheses as such.
